Our Story

Winnipeg's Real Saloon

Silver Spike Saloon is Winnipeg's home for cold beer, live entertainment, and that old-school western spirit. Whether you're rolling in after a long week or looking for a wild Saturday night — you've found your place.

Cold drafts on tap, shots at the bar, and a stage that never goes quiet. This is how the west was fun.
